# Privacy Policy – Busy at Work (@busy.at.work)

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The "igapp" application is used only by the owner of the Instagram account @busy.at.work to publish its own posts, reply to comments on its own posts, read statistics for its own account and run ads for its own account.

**What data we process**
- Content and statistics of the @busy.at.work account and its posts.
- Comments left on our posts (text, username), only so we can reply to them.

**What we do not do**
- We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
- We do not collect data from people who do not interact with @busy.at.work.
- We do not use data for any purpose other than running the @busy.at.work account.

**Data deletion**
To have your data (for example a comment) removed, delete it on Instagram or contact us via a direct message to @busy.at.work and we will remove any copy we hold within 30 days.

**Contact**
Direct message on Instagram: @busy.at.work
